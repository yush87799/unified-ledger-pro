# **App Name**: Unified Ledger Pro

## Core Features:

- Role Selection Gateway: Users choose their functional role (e.g., Business Owner, Admin) upon application entry, routing them to the appropriate tailored experience.
- Adaptive Dashboard Overviews: Each role is presented with a customized dashboard displaying relevant KPIs, summaries, and quick actions, tailoring insights to their operational focus.
- Product & Stock Management: A comprehensive module for managing product listings, tracking inventory levels, handling stock movements, and updating product details across locations.
- Automated Invoice & Billing System: Streamlined process for creating, managing, and tracking customer invoices, including automatic GST calculation (CGST, SGST, IGST), customer management, and payment status updates.
- GST Compliance & Reporting Tool: Provides tools for generating GST summaries and detailed financial reports, with an AI-powered tool for flagging potential discrepancies or areas for optimization in GST filings.
- Predictive Inventory & Demand Insights: An AI-driven tool that analyzes historical stock data to predict future demand, identifies potential low-stock scenarios, and suggests optimal reorder points.
- Dynamic UI Access Control Simulation: The user interface and navigation dynamically adjust based on the selected user role, simulating enterprise-level role-based access control without authentication.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary accent: A deep, modern violet-blue (#6633CC) to signify professionalism and functionality, used for interactive elements and brand accents.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated periwinkle (#EFEDF6) creating a clean and expansive canvas, complementing the primary while maintaining visual neutrality.
- Secondary accent: A soft, complementary sky blue (#84ACDB), derived from the primary hue, for visual distinction in secondary actions, charts, or highlights.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, slightly technical appeal, ensuring titles and key information stand out with clarity.
- Body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) chosen for its exceptional legibility and neutral design, ideal for data tables, detailed reports, and all primary content.
- Utilize a professional, consistent icon set, favoring outline or subtly filled styles, to intuitively guide users and reinforce actions without cluttering the UI.
- A clean, modular layout with rounded cards (8-16px radius), subtle shadows, and consistent spacing. Adherence to mobile-first principles ensures elements stack vertically on smaller screens, with the sidebar transforming into a bottom navigation or drawer.
- Incorporate subtle, purposeful animations including hover/tap effects on interactive components, smooth navigation transitions, loading skeletons, and brief toast notifications for a premium and highly responsive user experience.
